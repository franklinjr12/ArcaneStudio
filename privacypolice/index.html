    Privacy Policy

    Last updated: September 25, 2026

    Arcane Studio respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use games and services developed by Arcane Studio, including Beach Volleyball VR.

    Information We Process

    Arcane Studio does not require you to create an Arcane Studio account to play Beach Volleyball VR and does not intentionally collect personal information for advertising or profiling.

    When using online multiplayer features, technical information required to provide network communication, such as IP addresses and connection information, may be temporarily processed by our networking infrastructure and service providers.

    We do not sell personal information.

    How Information Is Used

    Technical information processed during online play is used only as necessary to:

    • establish and maintain multiplayer connections;
    • operate the game's online functionality;
    • maintain security and reliability;
    • diagnose technical problems.

    Arcane Studio does not use this information for targeted advertising or user profiling.

    Data Storage

    Arcane Studio does not intentionally maintain personal player profiles or permanent records of multiplayer network information unless technically necessary for operating or securing the service.

    Some infrastructure providers may temporarily process or retain technical logs as part of operating their services.

    Data Deletion

    You may request deletion of any personal information associated with you that Arcane Studio has collected or stored.

    To make a deletion request, contact Arcane Studio at arcane2dstudio@gmail.com. Include enough information for us to identify any data associated with your request.

    If Arcane Studio does not hold any personal information associated with you, we will inform you accordingly.

    Third-Party Platforms

    Beach Volleyball VR may be distributed through third-party platforms such as Meta Horizon and Steam. Those platforms may independently collect and process information according to their own privacy policies. Arcane Studio does not control the data-processing practices of those platforms.

    Changes to This Policy

    This Privacy Policy may be updated when the game, our services, or legal requirements change. The latest version will always be available on this page.
